If MicroFlame is meant to be an actual psilocybin microdose product, that's a different regulatory category (and different everything — sourcing, legality by state, claims) than the adaptogenic-mushroom framing used for Fairy Flame. Needs explicit confirmation, not inference from a mockup image.
